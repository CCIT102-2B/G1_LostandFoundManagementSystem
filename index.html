How FAITH FindIt works

For school use only · Three simple steps to help belongings find their way home.

STEP 01
Report it

Share the item name, a few details, and where it was last seen or found.

STEP 02
Search the board

Browse reports or use search and filters to find a possible match.

STEP 03
Update the status

When the item is reunited with its owner, mark the report as returned.
